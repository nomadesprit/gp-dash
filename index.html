Formula, assumptions & limitations

For one channel where S > T: ceil(N × (T − R) / (S − T)). Mixed plans estimate verified popup and campaign ratings separately, add their expected scores, then solve only the remaining gap.

AppFollow provides no effective per-country rating count. Popup acceptance is a store redirect—not a verified review—and popup stars are not external-store ratings. All manually entered conversion rates and scores are assumptions.
